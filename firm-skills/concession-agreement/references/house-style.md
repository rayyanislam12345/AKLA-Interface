# House drafting style

Measured from the firm's master and from two executed concession agreements it advised on (a 2020
expressway concession and a 2026 port corridor concession). Where the three disagree, the master
wins: it is the document the build fills, and a draft should read as one hand.

The skill never rewrites the master's prose. This note governs the **values the drafter supplies**,
so that a figure typed as `50%` lands on the page as `fifty percent (50%)` and a name lands with
the capitalisation the definitions expect.

## Numbers

Every count is written in words then figures: `thirty (30) days`, `twelve (12) months`, `two (2)
years`. Percentages the same: `fifty percent (50%)`, `one hundred and twenty five percent (125%)`.
Money carries the currency code, the figure with thousands separators, and the words in title
case:

> PKR 64,200,542,887/- (Pak Rupees Sixty Four Billion Two Hundred Million Five Hundred Forty Two
> Thousand Eight Hundred Eighty Seven)

The master's Schedule B uses `PKR [●] (Pakistani Rupees [●])`; the executed agreements use both
"Pakistani Rupees" and "Pak Rupees". Follow whatever the slot already says.

The build does this rendering. The intake asks for `30`, `50`, `9892542100` and each slot's role
decides the spelling (`words`, `numeral`, `pct_words`, `pct_numeral`, `pct_full`, `money_numeral`,
`money_words`). A value that is not a clean number is passed through untouched, so a drafter who
wants to write the phrase themselves can.

## Defined terms

Bold at the point of definition, unquoted, initial capitals on every significant word: `Scheduled
Substantial Completion Date`, `Development Performance Security Expiry Date`. In running text the
term appears in plain type with the same capitals. Single curly quotes mark a term being
introduced in prose: `means this ‘Concession Agreement’`, `shall not constitute a ‘Change in Law’`.

Capitalisation must match exactly. The master itself has `Change Of Scope Advance Percentage` in
Schedule B against `Change of Scope Advance Percentage` in Section 1.1, and the checker reports it
as an unmatched pointer. Do not introduce more of these.

Cross-reference formulae, in order of use in the master:

| Formula | Count in master |
|---|---|
| `shall have the meaning ascribed thereto in Schedule B (Specific Definitions)` | 142 |
| `shall bear the meaning ascribed thereto in …` | 7 |
| `has the meaning given to that term in …` | 1 |

Use the first. The executed agreements used `shall bear the meaning as ascribed thereto` throughout;
the master moved on.

## References

Sections and Articles are capitalised and carry their caption in brackets when first referred to in
a clause: `Section 10.3.3`, `Article 30 (Dispute Resolution)`, `Schedule L (Change in Control,
Change in Complete Control and Change in Shareholding Procedure)`. Schedules are lettered
`Schedule A` to `Schedule Y`; the schedule heading paragraph uses an en dash: `Schedule B – Specific
Definitions`.

## Dates and parties

Dates are `May 21, 2026`, month in words, day without a suffix, year in full. The execution line on
the cover is `Dated: as of` and in the preamble `entered into on this [Date] of [Month], [Year] at
[Location], Pakistan`. Parties are `the Agency` and `the Concessionaire`; the government behind the
Agency is named once in the preamble and again in Schedule B.

## Sentence habits

The master's clauses are long, single sentences built with a recognisable kit. Values supplied by
the drafter should not fight it:

- `provided, however, that` and `provided further, that` for carve-outs (49 uses)
- `Notwithstanding anything to the contrary set out herein` to open an overriding clause (14 uses)
- `inter alia` (11 uses), `in each case`, `as the case may be`, `from time to time`
- list limbs end `;` with `; and` or `; or` before the last, and the chapeau ends with a colon
- American spelling for `-ize` words (`utilization`, `authorize`), British elsewhere

## Footnotes

The master's footnotes are working notes, not part of the agreement. The build removes the 102
internal ones (`Input required from ADB…`, `This point is taken from Hubcanal Concession
Agreement…`) and keeps the 18 substantive notes and the 8 `[Conditional Amendment — …]`
instructions until each is resolved. Never leave an internal note in an issued draft.
