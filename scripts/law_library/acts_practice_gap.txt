# Acts the firm's actual work needs that the library was missing.
#
# Derived from the firm's own filing on the My Passport drive — in particular
# its curated "Laws", "Laws As Applicable To PR in Regards PPP Projects",
# "STZ Law", "EPZ Law", "SEZ Law" and "Mining Laws" folders — cross-checked
# against the 63 Acts already ingested. The library was strong on federal
# commercial law and almost empty on the things the firm actually does every
# day: provincial PPP, public procurement, railways, ports, land acquisition,
# special zones and mining.
#
# Used with: python3 scrape.py --scan --acts-file acts_practice_gap.txt
# Names are best-effort formal titles; the scraper's AI candidate picker
# resolves near-misses and rejects amendment-only or repealed versions.
