# Simplifying the site: what to remove, merge, hide or automate

Review of 18 October 2026: six readers over navigation, concepts, AI entry points, data management, admin surfaces and the chat server, with live usage counts; two critics (a new associate, the firm owner); one cut list. Case law excluded. Client names are not recorded here.

## Summary

- Today an associate sees 13 sidebar items, a project page with nine cards, and five doors into the AI with a slash palette, a plus menu and 40 document types to pick from.
- Live use says most of it is empty: Parties 0 rows, Notes 0, Project Context 1 blank row, Stages 1 of 72 done, timeslips 12 rows all on one test day, support requests 0, WhatsApp 0 linked, Summarise 0 messages.
- Six people have used the AI (63 chats, 91 documents) and one person is half of it; 1 sign-in in the last 7 days, 4 in 30. The site is not hard, it is too much to look at, and nothing in it arrives unless an associate acts.
- The shape after the cuts: four sidebar items (AI, Projects, Library, Settings); a project page that is files plus Ask AI; one chat with three verbs (Draft from the standard, Review, Edit) and plain sentences for everything else; admin tools under Settings.
- Cheap first, about a week: role-gate six pages, delete the empty cards, fold Dashboard and Clients into Projects, make + attach-only, drop the 20-second research step before every reply. Nothing is lost.
- Then stop the typing: document type from the index card (23 suggestions already waiting), version label and status from the file name, relevant laws resolved in the background, meeting outputs saved straight into the project.
- The biggest gain nobody had proposed: the index card already holds a summary, parties, key dates and laws for all 31 files and nothing shows it. Show it on the file and the project header.
- Three things would make the Hub worth opening: files arriving by email, AI work saved to the project by default, and a home that opens on what arrived this week.
- Keep: a small Tasks list, the Timesheet page as the record, Agreement as a deliberate action, and the old review panel until the new in-file review has run once for real.
- Do not delete: redline_suggestions (the only record of the firm's 9 AI reviews and 8 decisions), ingest-documents (called live by the law path), the skill zips (one holds the ITB master), the 37 needs_upload law rows (the library's to-do list).
- Before any cut: export the tables that go, and close four holes: any member can delete any Act, precedent, artifact or skill, and personal chats are private only by a client-side filter.
- Decisions only you can make are at the end: tasks, mandates, timesheet policy, email intake, who may delete what, and whether to accept the VM as the single point of failure.

## What each page is for

**AI Assistant (home, /ai).** Opens on *what arrived*: files that landed this week and what changed in them, reviews waiting, law updates that touch a project, and "+ Record a meeting". Below it, one box with a paper-clip. Three buttons: *Draft from the firm's standard* (the two types that have one), *Review*, *Edit*. Three example sentences that fill the box. No palette, no skills menu, no mode in the URL.

**Chats (/ai, left list).** One list of chats grouped by project; a project chip on the composer. Personal chats (no project) visible to the author and admins only. Rename and delete in the row menu; nothing else.

**Project view (/ai/projects/:id).** Header written by the engine from the index cards: one-line summary, parties, key dates, the laws the assistant is using, client, lead. Then the files, each reading "v3 · For partner review · 26 Sep 2026", with a filter box. A small Tasks list. One button: Ask. No stages, parties, notes, context, time or WhatsApp cards.

**Document window.** Whatever is open is the working document. Typing a change edits it; "review this" reviews it; changes land in the Word file as tracked changes with one Changes list (Waiting / Decided). One **Send out** button: next version, house-grammar name, clean and tracked copies, marked Circulated.

**Projects (/matters).** The list with a client column; New Project asks for a name and lets you type a client; everything else under More.

**Library.** The firm's past agreements and the Acts, one list with a type filter and one search box (clause search by meaning; exact words first). Admins see the standards and the upload.

**Record Meeting.** Reached from the composer. Pick the project, Start/Stop, language, audio source, or upload a recording. Minutes, Proposal and Concept Note save into the project and open in its chat.

**Settings.** Name, password, two-step sign-in, sign out everywhere; for admins, the Firm section.

**Help.** One screen naming the four items and a mailto; everything else is a question to the assistant.

## The one daily loop

1. A file arrives (email forward or drop-in). The Hub reads it, types it, labels it from its name, resolves the Acts it cites, and shows the card. Nobody picks a type, sets a status or tags a version.
2. The associate opens the project, sees what changed, opens the file in the assistant and says what they want: a draft from the standard, a review, an edit, a question.
3. The result is in the Word file with one Changes list; they accept or reject.
4. **Send out**: next version, firm's name, clean and tracked copies, Circulated.
5. At the end of the day the assistant drafts the timesheet from what it saw; they correct it and get the firm's .docx.

Four sidebar items, three verbs, one document window, one list of changes. The words that no longer appear: matter, transaction, taxonomy, standardise, master, indexed, searchable, passages, tag, scope, skill, mode, artifact, verify, agreement-as-a-mode, parties, stages, on hold, needs upload, Dashboard.

## Decisions for the owner

- [ ] Tasks: keep a small list on the project (two readers and the partner critic) or hide it (navigation reader)? Only 9 of 25 rows were made through the app and none since 12 Sep. Ask whether anyone relies on the 25 open ones.
- [ ] Mandate Opportunities: who should see it (founder/admin only, or senior counsel too), and is a weekly 'new tenders' message to those people enough to take the page off everyone else's sidebar? A partner may read it by eye today; nothing records views.
- [ ] Timesheet policy: is the daily timesheet still required of every associate (12 rows ever, all on one test day)? If yes, should the assistant draft it from chat, file and Timekeeper segments, and should the page leave the sidebar once that works? Hiding the only working producer first would remove the firm's one way to make the document.
- [ ] Email intake: do you want a firm mailbox (or per-project address) that drops attachments into the Hub? It is the one change that puts files in without an associate acting, and the confidentiality rule for it (whose mailbox, who can forward) is yours to set.
- [ ] Front door: should / land on the assistant's 'what arrived' home, or on Projects until the /ai workspace ships?
- [ ] Edge fallback: confirm VITE_CHAT_API_URL is set on the Vercel deploy, and accept that after the fallback is deleted the VM is the single point of failure (a clear 'the assistant is down' message instead of a silently older assistant).
- [ ] Legacy reviews: migrate the 137 suggestions into change records, or keep the old panel read-only until the 9 reviews are closed and then archive? Either way the rows are exported first.
